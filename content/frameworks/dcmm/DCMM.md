## DCMM Overview

**DCMM（数据管理能力成熟度评估模型，Data Management Capability Maturity Assessment Model）** 是用于评价和改进组织数据管理能力的国家标准。现行版本为 **GB/T 36073—2025（DCMM 2.0）**，自 2026 年 7 月 1 日实施。

### Key Characteristics

- **Current standard**: GB/T 36073—2025 "Data management capability maturity assessment model"
- **Effective date**: July 1, 2026
- **Structure**: 9 capability domains and 33 capability items
- **Major update**: Adds Data Assets and renames Data Application to Data Application and Circulation
- **Purpose**: Guide organizations in developing comprehensive data management capabilities
- **Application**: Enterprise data management assessment and certification
